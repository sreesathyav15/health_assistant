Smart Health Guidance for Rural Communities

An AI-powered assistant that helps you understand your symptoms, get first-aid guidance, and know when to seek medical help.

Key Features

Voice & Text Input

Speak or type your symptoms in Tamil or English. No keyboard skills needed.

AI Symptom Checker

Get intelligent suggestions about possible common illnesses with clear disclaimers.

Care Recommendation

Know whether to self-care, visit a clinic, or go to the hospital immediately.

First-Aid Guidance

Step-by-step guidance for burns, cuts, fever, snake bites, and heat stroke.

Medicine Reminders

Never miss your medication. Get timely alerts and reminders.

Offline Support

Access basic health guidance even in areas with poor internet connectivity.
